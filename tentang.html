EduTech Nusantara Digital merupakan perusahaan yang bergerak di bidang digitalisasi, tata kelola, dan penguatan kelembagaan pendidikan dengan fokus pada Pondok Pesantren, Madrasah, Sekolah, Yayasan Pendidikan, dan Perguruan Tinggi.

EduTech Nusantara Digital is a company engaged in the digitalization, governance, and institutional strengthening of education, with a focus on Boarding Schools, Madrasahs, Schools, Educational Foundations, and Universities.

Kami menyadari bahwa di era transformasi digital, lembaga pendidikan dituntut tidak hanya memiliki bangunan fisik, tetapi juga identitas digital yang profesional, sistem tata kelola (SOP) yang terstandar, serta kemampuan menyampaikan informasi secara transparan.

We realize that in the era of digital transformation, educational institutions are required to not only have physical buildings but also a professional digital identity, standardized governance systems (SOPs), and the ability to convey information transparently.

Kami hadir sebagai konsultan dan mitra strategis melalui layanan terintegrasi: pembuatan website resmi, penyusunan SOP, digital branding, pengembangan sistem informasi pendidikan, hingga pendampingan kelembagaan.

We serve as consultants and strategic partners through integrated services: official website development, SOP drafting, digital branding, development of educational information systems, and institutional mentoring.
